Last updated: November 17, 2026

The app

Longform blocks the YouTube Shorts player on Android. It needs an active subscription (or free trial) to block. Longform is not affiliated with YouTube or Google; YouTube may change its app at any time, which can stop blocking until we update Longform. Tell us if Shorts get through (Settings → "Report Shorts that got through").

Subscriptions

Yearly: 7 days free, then charged once a year. Monthly: charged once a month, no free trial. Prices are shown in the app — on the first screen — and in Google Play before you confirm, and may differ by country.

Payment is charged to your Google Play account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (free trial included).

If you allow notifications, we remind you two days before a free trial turns into a paid year.

Cancel anytime in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from Longform → Settings → "Manage or cancel in Google Play". Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.
