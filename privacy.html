Longform — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Longform blocks the YouTube Shorts player on Android and leaves the rest of YouTube alone. It is published by ikistudio. Longform is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.

What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. Longform has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own.

The only part of the app that uses the internet is Google's Play Billing Library, which Google requires for subscriptions. It sends your purchase and subscription status (purchase history) to Google Play so the app can check whether you are subscribed, and it may send technical diagnostics about the billing connection to Google. This data goes to Google, not to us, and we do not sell or share it.

Accessibility service

Longform uses Android's Accessibility service to do its one job: recognise the YouTube Shorts player and press Back for you.

  • What it reads: the structure of the screen (view identifiers) only while the YouTube app is in front. The service is limited to the YouTube app in its configuration; it does not receive events from any other app.
  • What it does: when the Shorts player appears, it performs the Back action and shows a short note. It does not type, tap, scroll or change anything else.
  • What it keeps: nothing about what you watch. The only thing stored is a count of how many times it stepped in today, on your phone, shown on the home screen. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Your control: the app explains this and asks for your agreement before you turn the service on. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility → Longform.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (whether you agreed to the Accessibility explanation, the trial reminder switch), the "Allow 5 minutes" timer and today's block count are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy. The app asks Google Play only whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows a local reminder two days before a free trial ends, and a countdown while "Allow 5 minutes" is running. Both are created on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service — to recognise the YouTube Shorts player (see above).
  • Notifications — for the trial reminder and the 5-minute countdown, only if you allow it.
  • Internet and Google Play billing — used only by Google's Play Billing Library for subscriptions (see above).

Children

Longform is not directed at children under 18.
